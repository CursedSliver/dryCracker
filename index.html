What is a seed?


In Cookie Clicker, a game seed is a combination of 5 letters that determines certain random events. It is useful for predicting certain events, primarily outcomes from the Force the Hand of Fate spell in the Grimoire, using the FtHoF planner website.
Your game seed and its associated predictions are normally accessible by exporting your save and inputting it into the website; however, in the new console release of Cookie Clicker, the export save buttons have been removed. Luckily, you can still "crack" and obtain your seed anyways by analyzing certain aspects of the game, such as your grandmas, which is what this website does. You can then import your 5-letter string into your planner directly to get your predictions.
Note that your seed resets upon wiping save or ascending!
